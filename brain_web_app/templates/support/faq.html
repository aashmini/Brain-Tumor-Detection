Frequently Asked Questions

Model & Accuracy
How accurate is the NeuroScan AI?
Our AI model achieves over 90% accuracy in detecting brain tumors using MRI scans, thanks to extensive training and fine-tuning on a well-labeled dataset.
Which model is used?
We use a pre-trained MobileNetV2 deep learning model that is optimized for medical image classification.
How was the model trained?
The model was trained on a publicly available dataset of brain MRI scans using supervised learning and fine-tuned using transfer learning techniques to enhance performance.
Usage & Platform
How to upload an MRI?
Simply click the "Test Your Scan" button on the home page and upload an image file in PNG, JPG, or JPEG format.
Is my data secure?
Yes, all uploaded data is processed securely. We do not store any personal or medical data without your explicit consent.
Can doctors use this for diagnosis?
NeuroScan is a supportive tool for early detection and should be used alongside professional medical advice. It is not a replacement for clinical diagnosis.
